# Samadhan Landing Page — Reference Specification

## Chosen Direction: Reference-Matched Civic Editorial

The supplied Samadhan screenshot is the ground-truth specification. The implementation will reproduce its restrained civic-institutional character: a full-bleed Jharkhand waterfall scene, a warm off-white serif display, compact monospaced navigation, burnt-orange action elements, and an analytical metrics band.

### Design Movement

Contemporary **editorial public-service identity**, combining environmental photography with the calm authority of a civic annual report.

### Core Principles

1. Maintain the reference’s deliberate asymmetry: the hero message stays low and left while the landscape carries the right half of the frame.
2. Use a dark, forest-toned photographic treatment with a controlled gradient overlay so every line remains legible.
3. Contrast expressive high-contrast serif headlines with disciplined, letter-spaced labels and controls.
4. Keep geometry quietly architectural: thin rules, square-cornered buttons, and vertical metric dividers.

### Color Philosophy

The supplied waterfall image retains its natural greens and stone blues while a deep evergreen overlay adds solemnity and visual continuity. Ivory typography creates measured contrast; a single oxidised-saffron orange marks calls to action and is reserved for high-intent interactions.

### Layout Paradigm

The page is a single cinematic viewport in which navigation forms a narrow top strip, the hero copy anchors to the lower-left field, the scroll cue balances the bottom-right, and a paper-coloured metrics ledger establishes the next section below the fold...

### Signature Elements

- A hairline navigation rule spanning the photograph.
- An overlaid forest gradient that deepens to near-black evergreen along the left and bottom edges.
- Tall metric numerals separated by warm grey vertical rules.

### Interaction Philosophy

Controls feel solid and decisive: a short lift on hover and a subtle press-down on activation. Navigation links use an understated underline reveal rather than decorative effects.

### Animation

On supported devices, hero labels, heading, copy, actions, and scroll cue fade up over a 260–420ms stagger. All animations honor reduced-motion preferences. Hover transitions use a crisp custom ease-out below 200ms.

### Typography System

Cormorant Garamond is the display face for the wordmark, headline, and metrics; IBM Plex Mono supports compact uppercase navigation, labels, and buttons. Display text is visually generous, while all utility text is tracked for civic-institutional precision.

### Brand Essence

**Samadhan is a civic reporting platform that makes Jharkhand’s everyday problems visible and actionable.** Personality: **considered, grounded, resolute**.

### Brand Voice

Headlines are observant and human; calls to action are specific and direct.

> “Every problem deserves a place to be seen.”

> “Report a challenge.”

### Wordmark & Logo

The wordmark is a sharply letter-spaced, all-caps Cormorant Garamond mark. The supplied reference contains no standalone pictorial logo; preserving that typographic wordmark is essential to fidelity.

### Signature Brand Color

**Samadhan Ember — #D84A1B**

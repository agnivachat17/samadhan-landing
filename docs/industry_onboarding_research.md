# Industry Onboarding Field Rationale

For Samadhan’s industry pathway, the onboarding form should collect five categories of information: **organization identity**, **authorized contacts**, **social-impact and support capabilities**, **governance/compliance confirmation**, and **commitment capacity**.

| Field group | Fields to capture in Samadhan | Reason for inclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Organization identity | Legal/business name, organization type, sector, website, registered location, operating districts | Establishes who the partner is and enables routing by sector and geography. |
| Authorized contacts | Partnership lead, designation, work email, telephone number, secondary finance/CSR contact | Partner onboarding should involve the people who can coordinate, decide, and support implementation. |
| Support capability | Preferred support modes, technical expertise, volunteer/mentor availability, facilities/equipment, in-kind contribution types | Corporate social-impact partnerships can support communities with funding, technical solutions, resources, training, and specialist capability. |
| Alignment and operating model | Priority domains, target beneficiaries, preferred project stage, expected milestones, collaboration preferences | Effective onboarding clarifies joint goals, roles, expectations, and performance measures before delivery begins. |
| Governance and confidence | CSR/impact policy URL, compliance confirmation, acknowledgement of data and brand guidelines | Partner onboarding guidance identifies legal/compliance and clear rules of engagement as early requirements. |

The dynamic industry form will therefore use: legal name, organization type, sector, website, registered location, partnership lead and CSR/impact lead details, capability areas, support modes, priority domains, geographic focus, budget or in-kind capacity band, preferred engagement stage, and compliance acknowledgements. It will not request unnecessary financial account details.
